[INFO] DNSSEC Not Enabled (CWE-345)
What: DNSSEC is not configured for secscan.us. Without DNSSEC, DNS responses can be forged or tampered with in transit (DNS cache poisoning / Kaminsky attack). Attackers can redirect your domain's traffic to malicious servers without users or servers being able to detect it.
Fix: Enable DNSSEC through your domain registrar. Most major registrars (Cloudflare, AWS Route 53, Google Domains) offer one-click DNSSEC. Your DNS hosting provider must also support signed zones. This requires coordination between your registrar and DNS provider.
Evidence: No DNSKEY records found for secscan.us (NOERROR (name exists)).
